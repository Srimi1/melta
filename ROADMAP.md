# Roadmap

Melta is built in stages. Each stage adds one self contained module under
`app/src/main/java/com/melta/app/<module>/` and a card on the home screen.

## Stage 1: Battery Bar ✅

- Status bar overlay with a Pokémon companion that rides the battery level
- All 1025 Pokémon bundled for offline use, crisp scaling, colors matched to the companion
- Type matched charging effects, full charge celebration, low battery mood
- Pixel art editor for custom companions and bar patterns, image import
- Low memory overlay service, boot persistence

## Stage 2: Notification shade

- Themed backgrounds and accents for notifications
- Companion that appears when the shade is pulled down

## Stage 3: Lock screen

- Custom clocks and companions on the always on display and lock screen

## Stage 4: Live wallpapers

- Battery and time aware animated wallpapers, same low power engine as the bar

## Stage 5: Icon studio

- Restyle home screen icons and shortcuts

Have an idea for a stage? [Open a feature request](https://github.com/Srimi1/melta/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
